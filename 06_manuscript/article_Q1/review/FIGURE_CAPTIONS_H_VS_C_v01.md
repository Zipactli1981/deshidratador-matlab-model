# Figure captions for H-vs-C objective geometry

## Figure 1 — main manuscript

**Three-objective geometry of the 18 evaluated solutions under corrected COST-E3D.** H denotes the nine historical R1 decision vectors reevaluated under the corrected formulation; C denotes the nine solutions generated directly under it. Both sets were internally nondominated, while their union contained 15 Rank 1 solutions (H=8, C=7). The plotted clouds describe finite evaluated sets and are not an independently verified reference surface.

## Figure S1 — supplementary

**Final moisture ratio versus modeled specific operating-energy cost.** C has a lower median final moisture ratio and a moderately higher median specific operating-energy cost than H. Marginal intervals are observed finite-set ranges; no statistical inference is implied.

## Figure S2 — supplementary

**Final moisture ratio versus modeled specific operational greenhouse-gas emissions.** The emissions indicator combines direct LPG-combustion CO2 and indirect grid-electricity CO2e per kilogram of water removed. It is not a life-cycle carbon footprint, a complete greenhouse-gas inventory, or total environmental impact.

## Figure S3 — supplementary

**Modeled specific operating-energy cost versus modeled specific operational greenhouse-gas emissions.** H and C remain interleaved within shared marginal ranges. The figure represents the evaluated finite sets and does not establish statistical separation or global superiority.
