# COST-E3D-R2G: Controlled reevaluation of existing R1 finite solutions memo v96z

## 1. Purpose

This memo records the controlled direct reevaluation of exactly the nine existing finite solutions from the historical R1 artifact using the corrected productive tri-objective function. The exercise checks the implemented objective identities at those fixed points. It is not a new optimization, does not generate new decision vectors, and does not re-establish a Pareto front.
